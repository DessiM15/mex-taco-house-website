About Us

Welcome to Mex Taco House! We're a family-owned restaurant bringing authentic Mexican flavors to Cypress, TX. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, using fresh ingredients and traditional cooking methods.

From our signature tacos to our famous chilaquiles, every dish is made with love and care. Come experience the taste of real Mexican cuisine!
